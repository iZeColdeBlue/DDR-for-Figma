Rules

The strip names what happened, in at most five words. The body says what to do.

Strip: status colour with navy icon and title. Side padding 8px in the strip and the body.

Link: the standard link style (secondary, underlined). Arrow only when it opens outside the plugin.

Icon shape tells the status apart without colour: circle with tick, circle with i, triangle, octagon.
